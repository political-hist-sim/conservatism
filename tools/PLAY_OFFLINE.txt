Conservatism: An Alternate History - offline copy
==================================================

You don't need the internet or anything installed to play this copy.

1. Unzip conservatism-offline.zip somewhere you'll keep it, such as your
   Documents folder.
2. Open the "conservatism" folder.
3. Double-click index.html. It opens in your web browser (Chrome, Edge,
   Firefox or Safari) and the game starts.

Saved games
-----------
Your browser keeps the saves for this copy, separately from the online
version. Keep the folder in the same place, and don't clear your browser's
history "for all time" with "site data" ticked, or the saves go with it.

Updating
--------
Download the zip again from https://political-hist-sim.github.io/conservatism/conservatism-offline.zip
and replace the old "conservatism" folder with the new one, in the same place.
Saves from an older version may not work with a newer one.
